# Morning Brief — 2026-07-30

**Two actively-exploited network-appliance flaws force patch action this week: the Arista VeloCloud CVE-2026-16812 federal remediation deadline (CVSS 10.0) expires today, and CISA just KEV-listed an actively-exploited Cisco Secure FMC flaw (CVE-2026-20316).**

**Why it matters:** SD-WAN orchestrators and firewall management consoles are fleet-wide control points across ITAR-regulated DIB networks; an unauthenticated foothold on either is a management-plane-takeover-class exposure carrying confirmed exploitation and a federal clock.

## 🚨 Active Threats

**UPDATE: Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator CVE-2026-16812 federal remediation deadline expires today (CVSS 10.0, actively exploited)**
- What: The accelerated CISA KEV deadline (dueDate 2026-07-30) for the actively-exploited SD-WAN control-plane flaw lands today; no new substance since the 2026-07-28 patch/CVSS-10.0/attacker-IP disclosure. Orchestrator compromise implies fleet-wide SD-WAN control — unpatched DIB fleets carry federal non-compliance plus standing exploitation risk.
- Action: Confirm VCO is on a fixed build (5.2.3.14 / 6.1.3.4 / 6.4.2.4 / 7.0.0.1) today; hunt the three published attacker IPs.
- Source: [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely (single-source veto)
- 🔗 **Update on:** 2026-07-29 morning brief — deadline moved from T-1 to today.

**Cisco Secure FMC hard-coded-credential flaw CVE-2026-20316 is actively exploited; Cisco has patched**
- What: CISA KEV-listed CVE-2026-20316 (Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center) on 2026-07-29 with a 2026-08-01 deadline; a hard-coded/static credential (CWE-798) lets a remote unauthenticated attacker log in to the FMC console. CVSS 5.3.
- Why it matters for A&D: FMC is the centralized firewall management plane in large ITAR/DIB datacenters; unauthenticated console access chains toward privilege escalation.
- Action: Apply Cisco's 2026-07-29 patch before the August 1 KEV deadline; retrieve Cisco's published detection IOCs.
- Source: [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/cisco-secure-fmc-zero-day-exploited-in-the-wild/) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely (single-source veto)
- Related: CVE-2026-20316 (net-new; distinct from March-2026 CVE-2026-20079 / CVE-2026-20131)

## 🔓 Vulnerabilities

**Broadcom patches critical VMware vCenter auth-bypass + RCE (CVE-2026-59309 / CVE-2026-59310, CVSS 9.8) and an ESXi VM-escape**
- What: VMSA-2026-0006 (2026-07-29) fixes two unauthenticated, network-reachable vCenter flaws (Directory Service auth bypass + Syslog directory-traversal RCE) plus ESXi VM-escape CVE-2026-47876. Broadcom reports no in-the-wild exploitation at disclosure.
- Why it matters for A&D: ESXi/vCenter is the dominant DIB-datacenter virtualization stack; this CVSS-9.8 unauthenticated pair is likely weaponized quickly post-patch (including by ransomware crews). The absence of reported exploitation is point-in-time and vendor-awareness-bounded, not settled fact — patch now.
- Source: [Rapid7](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-critical-vmware-vcenter-vulnerabilities-allow-authentication-bypass-and-remote-code-execution-cve-2026-59309-cve-2026-59310) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/critical-vm-escape-vulnerability-patched-in-vmware-esxi/) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely (single-source veto)

**Rapid7 flags critical unauthenticated RCE in all JetBrains TeamCity On-Premises versions (CVE-2026-63077, CVSS 9.8) — patch available**
- What: An unauthenticated deserialization flaw in the agent-polling protocol lets a remote attacker run OS commands as the TeamCity server process and read stored credentials; JetBrains patched it 2026-07-27, reports no exploitation at disclosure, all On-Premises versions affected.
- Why it matters for A&D: A CI/CD build-server RCE yielding credential theft and pipeline-integrity loss is a direct software-supply-chain risk to an ITAR-regulated SDLC. Rapid7 notes prior TeamCity auth-bypass CVEs were weaponized quickly post-disclosure; near-term exploitation is likely — patch now. (Thinnest sourcing of the three.)
- Source: [Rapid7](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-cve-2026-63077-critical-unauthenticated-remote-code-execution-in-jetbrains-teamcity) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely (single-source veto)

# Morning Brief — 2026-07-25

**Two CISA KEV-listed flaws hit their federal remediation deadline today** — Check Point SmartConsole (CVE-2026-16232) and on-prem Microsoft SharePoint (CVE-2026-50522), both actively exploited for authentication-token theft. Today is the last day to remediate before federal non-compliance and standing exploitation risk.

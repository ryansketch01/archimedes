# Ivanti EPMM CVE-2026-6973 — Shadowserver tags 800+ exposed appliances; third exploited EPMM CVE in 4 months

## Summary

BleepingComputer published "CISA gives feds four days to patch Ivanti flaw exploited as zero-day" on 2026-05-08T12:16 UTC, adding three layers of operational context to the existing A1 Ivanti EPMM CVE-2026-6973 finding (finding-2026-05-08-0002, this morning). New material: (1) Shadowserver telemetry indicates 800+ Ivanti EPMM appliances exposed online; (2) federal patch deadline framed as "midnight Sunday, May 10" (2026-05-11T00:00 EDT — 4 calendar days from KEV listing); (3) recurrence pattern — CVE-2026-6973 is the third exploited EPMM CVE in 2026 following CVE-2026-1281 (January) and CVE-2026-1340 (April).

This finding does not re-grade the underlying CVE / KEV-listing / exploitation-status facts (those are A1-corroborated in finding-2026-05-08-0002). It grades the supplementary operational telemetry at B2 with single-source veto applied.

## Technical detail

**Shadowserver exposure count:** ~800+ Ivanti EPMM appliances internet-reachable. This is internet-reachable count only — internal-only or NAT-protected EPMM deployments are not surveyable but follow similar patch-discipline curves in practice.

**Recurrence pattern (per BleepingComputer citing prior CyberScoop coverage):**
- CVE-2026-1281 — January 2026 EPMM CVE, exploited
- CVE-2026-1340 — April 2026 EPMM CVE, exploited
- CVE-2026-6973 — May 2026 EPMM CVE, exploited (current)

Three exploited Ivanti EPMM CVEs in four months establishes a serial-target pattern that should inform vuln-tracker's Ivanti-EPMM dossier posture. Per Hard Rule 2: prior-CVE attribution context (CyberScoop noted "China- and Iran-attributed groups" exploited the earlier two CVEs) is recorded as HISTORICAL context for the prior CVEs, NOT transferred to CVE-2026-6973. CVE-2026-6973 attribution remains unclaimed.

**Ivanti's own statement (per BleepingComputer paraphrase):** Ivanti is aware of "very limited exploitation" of CVE-2026-6973 (admin authentication required for successful exploitation). Ivanti recommends credential rotation on EPMM appliances previously suspected of exploitation.

**Patches:** Versions 12.6.1.1, 12.7.0.1, 12.8.0.1 (already documented in finding-2026-05-08-0002).

## A&D-target relevance

Ivanti EPMM is a mobile device management platform widely deployed in regulated environments — federal civilian, DoD contractors, A&D primes for employee mobile device management including BYOD/CYOD configurations. ITAR-regulated estates may use EPMM in classified-network-adjacent roles. The 800+ internet-exposed count is a partial proxy for total install base; internal-only deployments are larger and follow similar patch-discipline curves.

The serial-target pattern (three CVEs in four months) elevates Ivanti EPMM as a sustained adversary-attention surface. Patch-cadence discipline becomes operational-risk reduction at a higher rate than for one-off-vulnerable products.

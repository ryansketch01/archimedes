# ISC BIND 9 CVE-2026-3593 — DNS-over-HTTPS heap use-after-free; NVD 9.8 vs ISC vendor 7.4 CVSS divergence (third such instance this week); vendor explicit "memory corruption does NOT yield RCE" and "no active exploits"; patched BIND 9.20.23 / 9.21.22; DNS-resolver-class adjacency to morning finding-0005 Unbound dual criticals

## Summary

NVD and Internet Systems Consortium (vendor-authority-on-own-product) jointly disclose CVE-2026-3593, a heap use-after-free in BIND 9's DNS-over-HTTPS handling triggered by crafted HTTP/2 traffic to a DoH endpoint. NVD scores 9.8 CRITICAL on inherent network-exploitability and full CIA impact; ISC scores 7.4 High with high attack complexity (AC:H) and no integrity impact (I:N), reflecting vendor's direct knowledge that crafted HTTP/2 traffic patterns are non-trivial to construct and that the bug does not yield attacker-controlled memory writes. ISC explicitly states the bug enables "memory corruption" but "does NOT result in remote code execution." Affected BIND 9.20.0-9.20.22 and 9.21.0-9.21.21; patched BIND 9.20.23 / 9.21.22. Vendor explicitly states "we are not aware of any active exploits." No KEV listing. No actor attribution. Workaround: disable DoH functionality. This finding lands the same week as morning finding-0005 (NLnet Labs Unbound dual criticals) — same network-infrastructure layer, different open-source-vendor lineage — and is the third CNA-vendor-vs-NIST-uplift instance observed in 24h (lesser divergence, same direction).

## Technical detail

### CVE-2026-3593 — BIND 9 DoH heap UAF

- **CVE**: CVE-2026-3593
- **NVD CVSS v3.1**: 9.8 CRITICAL — AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H
- **ISC vendor CVSS v3.1**: 7.4 High — AV:N/AC:H/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:H
- **CWE**: CWE-416 (Use After Free)
- **Trigger**: Crafted HTTP/2 traffic sent to a DNS-over-HTTPS endpoint
- **Affected components**: Both authoritative BIND 9 servers and recursive resolvers face impact if configured with DoH listening
- **Affected versions**: BIND 9.20.0 through 9.20.22; BIND 9.21.0 through 9.21.21; BIND 9.20.9-S1 through 9.20.22-S1 (Preview Edition for ISC subscribers)
- **Patched versions**: BIND 9.20.23; BIND 9.21.22; BIND 9.20.23-S1 (Preview)

- **Active exploitation**: Vendor explicit — "we are not aware of any active exploits." No KEV listing.
- **Impact class per vendor explicit statement**: Memory corruption — does NOT result in remote code execution. Vendor's I:N (integrity-none) scoring backs the explicit no-RCE attestation.

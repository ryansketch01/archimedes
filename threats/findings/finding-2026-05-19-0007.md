# Microsoft MSTIC + DCU disrupt Fox Tempest Malware-Signing-as-a-Service (MSaaS) operation — Artifact Signing abuse, 1,000+ fraudulent certificates revoked, U.S. District Court case unsealed

## Summary

Microsoft Threat Intelligence (MSTIC) and the Microsoft Digital Crimes Unit (DCU) disclosed a coordinated takedown of Fox Tempest, a financially-motivated threat actor operating a Malware-Signing-as-a-Service (MSaaS) offering that abused Microsoft Artifact Signing (formerly Azure Trusted Signing) to generate short-lived (~72-hour) fraudulent code-signing certificates. Microsoft revoked over 1,000 such certificates, took down hundreds of Azure tenants and subscriptions supporting the operation, and rendered the signspace[.]cloud platform defunct. A U.S. District Court case detailing the disruption was unsealed Tuesday 2026-05-19. The MSaaS was active since at least May 2025. SecurityWeek (Eduard Kovacs) and The Record relay MSTIC's findings without contributing independent telemetry. Downstream consumers of Fox Tempest's signed-malware service include Vanilla Tempest (Rhysida ransomware), Storm-0501, Storm-2561, and Storm-0249 — all Microsoft-taxonomy actor designations not currently on Archimedes _roster.yaml.

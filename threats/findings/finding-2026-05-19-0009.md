## Summary

SecurityWeek (Eduard Kovacs) relays a Drupal Pre-Security-Advisory (PSA) for a "highly critical" vulnerability with patches scheduled for 2026-05-20 (Wednesday) between 17:00 and 21:00 UTC across all supported branches (Drupal 11.3.x, 11.2.x, 10.6.x, 10.5.x). CVE ID is embargoed; CVSS is undisclosed; affected component and mechanism are undisclosed pending the 2026-05-20 patch release window.

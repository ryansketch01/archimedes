# Fortinet PSIRT — Critical Unauthenticated RCE Pair in FortiSandbox and FortiAuthenticator; No In-The-Wild Exploitation Observed

## Summary

Fortinet PSIRT shipped advisories on 2026-05-12 disclosing **two
Critical-severity unauthenticated vulnerabilities** — one in
**FortiSandbox** (the network-sandboxing / advanced-threat-protection
appliance, FG-IR-26-136 / **CVE-2026-26083**, missing-authorization on
the web GUI) and one in **FortiAuthenticator** (the multi-factor
authentication appliance, **CVE-2026-44277**, improper-access-control
allowing unauthenticated crafted-request code execution). Both are
patched at-disclosure; Fortinet states no in-the-wild exploitation
observed for either CVE. BleepingComputer's Sergiu Gatlan relays the
PSIRT disclosure with editorial context that "Fortinet vulnerabilities
are frequently exploited in ransomware and cyber-espionage attacks" —
historical-pattern observation, not a current ITW claim. No A&D prime
is named in the advisories.

# FCC Pushes Foreign-Router and Foreign-Drone Update-Ban Deadlines to 2029 (2026-05-11)

## Summary

The FCC's Office of Engineering and Technology on 2026-05-11 announced
that its ban on software and firmware updates from foreign-made router
and drone manufacturers will be pushed to a unified 2029-01-01 deadline,
extending the prior router deadline of 2027-03-01 and the prior drone
deadline of 2027-01-01 by approximately 22-25 months. The FCC cited
public-interest considerations on patch availability and unspecified
"national security concerns regarding overseas manufacturing"; no
specific foreign vendors are named in the announcement. The regulatory
framework is A&D-adjacent at capability-level only — counter-UAS
business segments at primes, DIB / CMMC foreign-vendor cyber-risk
posture parallel, and DJI / Chinese-vendor DoD-prohibition history all
intersect — but no named A&D primes, no tracked actors, no CVEs, no
IOCs are involved.

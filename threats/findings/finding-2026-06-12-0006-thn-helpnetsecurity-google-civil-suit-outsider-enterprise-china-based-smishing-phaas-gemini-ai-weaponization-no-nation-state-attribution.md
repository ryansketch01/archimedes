# Google files civil suit against China-based "Outsider" smishing PhaaS — Gemini AI weaponized for phishing-page HTML; 2.5M Android-targeted messages May-June 2026; 100k+ alleged victims; civil litigation not criminal indictment; NOT Chinese intelligence services

## Summary

Google filed a civil complaint in Manhattan federal court (Southern District of New York) against a China-based cybercrime network operating the "Outsider" phishing-as-a-service (PhaaS) platform — relayed by The Hacker News and Help Net Security on 2026-06-12. The complaint alleges a five-group operational structure (Developer, Data Broker, Spammer, Theft, Telegram coordination) selling licenses as low as $88/week via Telegram bot `@OutsiderCodeBot`, with 290+ pre-built templates impersonating legitimate institutions. Operators allegedly weaponized Gemini and other AI tools by prompting them to generate "gift redemption page" HTML, then pasted the output into Outsider to construct credential-theft sites. Google's complaint estimates 9,000 fake websites identified November 2025 to April 2026, 1.59M fraudulent URLs linked to the service, 2.5M smishing messages to Android users in May-June 2026 alone, and over 100,000 alleged victims. This is civil litigation, NOT criminal indictment — allegations are pleadings, not prosecutorial findings. No nation-state attribution: the complaint identifies "China-based criminal actors," NOT Chinese intelligence services. Outsider Enterprise is not in Archimedes' roster. Pairs with finding-2026-06-12-0007 (Tenet Agentjacking + LangGraph CVEs) as the AI-tooling weaponization cluster for this brief window.

## Technical detail

- **Defendant:** China-based cybercrime network operating "Outsider" PhaaS platform. Civil complaint pleading layer.
- **Five-group operational structure (alleged):** Developer (software/templates); Data Broker (target lists); Spammer (bulk messaging); Theft (monetization); Telegram (coordination/recruitment).
- **Service economics (alleged):** licenses as low as $88/week via Telegram bot `@OutsiderCodeBot`; 290+ pre-built templates mimicking legitimate institutions.
- **AI weaponization method (alleged):** operators instructed users to prompt Gemini (and other AI tools) with seemingly innocuous requests for HTML code to build "gift redemption pages." Prompts asked models to avoid JavaScript and use inline CSS. Generated code was then pasted into Outsider to construct fraudulent credential-theft websites.
- **Impact scope (alleged, per Google complaint):**
  - 100,000+ victims estimated.
  - Millions in financial losses.
  - 9,000 fake websites identified November 2025 to April 2026.
  - 1.59 million fraudulent URLs linked to the service.
  - 2.5 million messages sent to Android users in May-June 2026 alone.

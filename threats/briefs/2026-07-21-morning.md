# Morning Brief — 2026-07-21

**Arctic Wolf ties the Qilin ransomware operation to active exploitation of PAN-OS GlobalProtect CVE-2026-0257** — a critical unauthenticated auth-bypass Palo Alto patched May 13 and CISA added to KEV May 29 — as the initial-access vector in multiple June 2026 intrusions.

**Why it matters:** PAN-OS GlobalProtect is edge VPN infrastructure pervasive across ITAR/DIB primes and their supplier networks, so an unauthenticated bypass that establishes a VPN connection is an initial-access foothold at exactly the tier this program defends — now with a ransomware end-state attached.

## 🚨 Active Threats

**UPDATE: Qilin ransomware attributed to PAN-OS GlobalProtect CVE-2026-0257 exploitation**
- What: Arctic Wolf Labs, relayed by BleepingComputer, traced multiple distinct June 2026 intrusions — unauthenticated GlobalProtect auth-bypass to Qilin ransomware deployment — to CVE-2026-0257. Named victim sectors: automotive, beverage, healthcare, media, government. No A&D victim named.
- Keep the two legs distinct: active exploitation of CVE-2026-0257 is **very likely** — CISA KEV attests it independently (A2). The **Qilin attribution is likely** — Arctic Wolf is the sole originator, single-source veto applied (B2). The KEV grade does not corroborate the Qilin linkage.
- Why it matters for A&D: Shadowserver counts **167,000+ internet-exposed GlobalProtect instances**, patch status unknown for most — structural edge exposure, not A&D targeting.
- Action: Confirm PAN-OS GlobalProtect is on the May 13 fix; the CISA KEV federal deadline has already passed. No atomic IOCs were published — hunt on CVE/patch state, not indicators.
- Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/critical-globalprotect-vpn-bug-now-exploited-in-ransomware-attacks/) · Digraph: B2 (attribution) / A2 (exploitation)
- 🔗 **Update on:** CVE-2026-0257 (covered late May–early June, finding-2026-05-29-0004) — the material change is a ransomware attribution; CVSS remains unestablished and the CVE still has no repo dossier (vuln-tracker coverage-gap). Distinct from ZD-004 [CVE-2026-0300](threats/vulnerabilities/PAN-OS-CVE-2026-0300/profile.md) (User-ID pre-auth RCE) — different component.

## 🔓 Vulnerabilities

**UPDATE: ServiceNow CVE-2026-6875 exploitation claim narrows to a PoC replay**
- Defused walked back its in-the-wild report — the captured traffic matched a public proof-of-concept, not confirmed malicious activity, and ServiceNow attests hosted instances are unaffected.
- The exploitation observation stands at B2/**likely** — not retracted — and there is still no CISA KEV listing.
- Why it matters for A&D: on-prem ServiceNow operators should still apply the fix; the certainty on active exploitation dropped, the patch imperative did not.
- Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/critical-servicenow-code-execution-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks/) · Digraph: B2

**UPDATE: LegacyHive Windows profsvc LPE zero-day gains an unofficial micropatch**
- ACROS Security / 0patch shipped a micropatch. Still no CVE, no Microsoft patch, and no in-the-wild exploitation.
- Why it matters for A&D: ubiquitous Windows endpoint LPE is a ransomware and lateral-movement precursor; the unofficial fix gives defenders an interim option ahead of any vendor patch.
- Source: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/07/15/microsofts-serial-tormentor-drops-legacyhive-0-day/) · Digraph: B2 · Related: VT-042 [LegacyHive](threats/vulnerabilities/LegacyHive/profile.md)

**UPDATE: SonicWall SMA1000 CVE-2026-15409/15410 — incremental detail**
- The two KEV-listed flaws split into an SSRF (15409) and a command injection (15410); UTA0533 deploys new ROOTRUN tooling; affected models are 6210, 7210, and 8200v.
- The added detail does not lift the assessment — active exploitation holds at A2/**likely**.
- Why it matters for A&D: SMA1000 is remote-access edge infrastructure; operators should verify affected models against the fixed build.
- Source: [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · Digraph: A2

# Morning Brief — 2026-07-08

**CVE-2026-48282 — the max-severity Adobe ColdFusion RCE — is now CISA KEV-listed with a federal remediation deadline of Thursday, July 10.** NVD confirms CVSS 10.0; exploitation began within roughly two hours of disclosure.

**Why it matters:** ColdFusion is common in enterprise and contractor web stacks. An unauthenticated pre-auth RCE on an internet-exposed instance means full compromise with no credentials — a structural exposure for any DIB org running it.

## 🚨 Active Threats

**UPDATE: Adobe ColdFusion CVE-2026-48282 is CISA KEV-listed with a July 10 federal deadline**
- What: CISA added the max-severity unauthenticated ColdFusion RCE to the KEV catalog on July 7; NVD confirmed CVSS 10.0 (path traversal → arbitrary code execution, no auth, no user interaction). Adobe patched July 1 — this is an n-day, not a zero-day.
- Exploitation evidence (read honestly): CISA KEV is the sole directly-retrieved active-exploitation attestation. KEVIntel honeypot telemetry (first hits ~2h post-disclosure) and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security remain relay-conveyed through BleepingComputer and SecurityWeek — publisher-independent, but relays of the same CISA determination, not an independent exploitation basis. Active exploitation is very likely; this is not a robustly triangulated multi-source cross-match.
- Why it matters for A&D: ColdFusion's DIB footprint is structural (enterprise/contractor app servers); no named A&D victim, no sector targeting, no actor attributed. Shadowserver tracks ~800 internet-exposed instances.
- Action: Patch to Adobe's July 1 release now. Treat any unpatched internet-exposed instance as compromised pending IR. Federal remediation is due Thursday, July 10.
- Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-adobe-coldfusion-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks/) · [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · Digraph: A2 · WEP (exploitation): very likely

## 🔓 Vulnerabilities

Two more CVEs from the same July 7 CISA KEV batch — both actively exploited, both carrying the July 10 federal deadline, both low A&D relevance (monitoring items).

**Joomla page-builder RCE cluster — CVE-2026-48908 / CVE-2026-56290**
- What: CISA KEV-listed two unauthenticated file-upload-to-RCE flaws (CWE-434, CVSS 9.8 v3.1 / 10.0 v4.0) in the SP Page Builder and Page Builder CK Joomla extensions. Attackers plant hidden admin accounts and PHP backdoors, and dropped web shells within hours of the June 27 patch.
- A&D relevance: negligible — website-CMS plugins with no material industrial-base deployment path.
- Exploitation rests solely on CISA KEV; NVD confirms the CVE facts, not exploitation. Single-source veto holds the assessment at likely.
- Source: [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-48908) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely
- Related: CVE-2026-48908, CVE-2026-56290 (net-new; flagged for vuln-tracker addition)

**Langflow CVE-2026-55255 — authenticated IDOR, active exploitation**
- What: CISA KEV-listed an authorization-bypass flaw (CWE-639) in the Langflow AI-agent framework; an authenticated attacker executes another user's flow. Exploited since ~June 25. NVD CVSS v3.1 8.4 is authoritative — the 9.9 relay figure is most likely the CVSS v4.0 score, not a contradiction.
- A&D relevance: low — no DIB deployment pathway today; a watch item if AI-agent-framework exposure grows in the estate.
- Chain reference: CVE-2026-33017 (prior Langflow RCE, patched March 2026) — related, not cross-walked into this exploitation claim.
- Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-orders-feds-to-prioritize-patching-langflow-auth-bypass-flaw/) · [NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-55255) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely
- Related: CVE-2026-55255 (net-new; flagged for vuln-tracker addition)

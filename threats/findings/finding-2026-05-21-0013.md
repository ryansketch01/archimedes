## Technical detail

### Initial access vector shift (per Rapid7 Labs Q1 2026 telemetry)

- **Top IAV**: Vulnerability exploitation at 38% of total observed initial access
- **Surpassed**: Social engineering (previously #1 in prior Rapid7 quarterly reports)
- **Coupling fact**: Over 50% of exploited vulnerabilities used as IAVs in Q1 2026 were zero-click network-facing (no auth, no user interaction, no phishing payload — remote-exploit-against-exposed-service)
- **Rapid7 framing (paraphrased per Hard Rule 7)**: The shift indicates attackers are finding AI-enabled vulnerability exploitation easier to accomplish than human-targeting social-engineering operations

### Ransomware tactic shift

- **Observable**: "Marked shift towards 'pure extortion'" tactics (under 15-word quote limit)
- **Mechanism**: Threat actors increasingly prioritize rapid data exfiltration over traditional encryption-based attacks
- **Rationale per Rapid7**: Reduces operational risk (no encryption-payload-detection signature on victim endpoints); shortens dwell time; bypasses backup-and-restore recovery options that undermined encryption-based ransom leverage

- **Russian campaigns**: intelligence collection, telecommunications targeting
- **Chinese campaigns**: persistent access operations

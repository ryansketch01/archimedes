## Summary

Symantec Threat Hunter Team + Carbon Black published "Fast16: Pre-Stuxnet Sabotage Tool Was Built to Subvert Nuclear Weapons Simulations" on 2026-05-16, extending SentinelLABS' April 2026 originating research on a 2005-era simulation-sabotage framework.

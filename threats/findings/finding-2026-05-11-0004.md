# UK ICO Fines South Staffordshire Water £963.9K for 2022 Cl0p Breach (2026-05-11)

## Summary

The UK Information Commissioner's Office on 2026-05-11 fined South
Staffordshire Water £963,900 ($1.3M) over a 2022 Cl0p ransomware
intrusion that exposed personal data of 633,887 customers and employees.
The ICO's published timeline describes a 22-month dwell time —
September 2020 initial access via malicious email, escalation to domain
admin via ZeroLogon (CVE-2020-1472) in May 2022, discovery in July 2022,
and 4.1TB of stolen data published on Cl0p's leak site in August 2022.
The Cl0p attribution restates the 2022 leak-site claim and
contemporaneous vendor research; the ICO's 2026 action does not publish
new technical evidence. Sector is UK water utility (critical national
infrastructure), not A&D.

## Technical detail

- **Attack vector:** Initial access via malicious email attachment (September 2020); 20-month latent persistence; lateral movement via ZeroLogon (CVE-2020-1472) exploitation of unpatched Netlogon Remote Protocol weakness to compromise domain administrator account access (May 2022).
- **Discovery:** July 2022 — IT performance issues triggered investigation, ~22 months after initial access.
- **Data published:** August 2022 — 4.1TB on Cl0p leak site, including names, addresses, dates of birth, bank account numbers and sort codes, National Insurance numbers, and disability information for Priority Services Register customers (a vulnerable-population subset).
- **CVE context:** ZeroLogon (CVE-2020-1472) was disclosed August 2020 with patches available immediately; this incident represents long-dwell exploitation of an unpatched 2020 weakness, not a fresh vulnerability event. ZeroLogon is NOT in Archimedes _index.yaml (current scope is active 2026 threat-tracked CVEs).
- **Regulatory framing:** ICO action focuses on data protection failures (inadequate security controls allowing the long-dwell intrusion), not on threat-actor TTP analysis.

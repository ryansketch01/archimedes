# Cisco patches critical ASA / Firepower VPN flaw CVE-2026-20381 (CVSS 9.0); SANS reports mass scanning hours after advisory release

## Summary

Cisco released advisory cisco-sa-asa-ftd-vpn-rce-uYbV6Lkj on 2026-05-04 evening for CVE-2026-20381, a pre-authentication memory corruption in the ASA / Firepower Threat Defense (FTD) VPN session-handling path that yields unauthenticated RCE on the appliance. CVSS 9.0 per Cisco PSIRT; NVD entry confirms record. Cisco shipped fixed builds the same day: ASA 9.20.4.5 and 9.22.2.3; FTD 7.4.3.2 and 7.6.1.1. **No confirmed exploitation at advisory publication.** SANS Internet Storm Center reported a 5x increase in scanning activity against the standard ASA SSL-VPN logon page (`/+CSCOE+/logon.html`) within four hours of the advisory release — consistent with reconnaissance for exploitable instances, but no exploit attempt confirmed in SANS honeypots yet. Cisco ASA and Firepower are heavily deployed at primes and DIB suppliers as remote-access VPN concentrators; CMMC-aligned environments often expose these as the only authenticated path for offsite engineers. Patch priority is immediate even without confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.

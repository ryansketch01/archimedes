# Morning Brief — 2026-07-23

**Check Point shipped the fix for actively-exploited SmartConsole flaw [CVE-2026-16232](../vulnerabilities/CVE-2026-16232/profile.md) and published 5 attacker IPs — the zero-day-no-patch window is now closable, but it stays open on your estate until you deploy, against a federal deadline this Friday, July 25.**

**Why it matters:** SmartConsole sits on the security-management plane of many DIB estates, and an unauthenticated admin-token-theft primitive there is access-broker-grade. Patch-available is not patch-applied — exploitation is ongoing and the deadline is two days out.

## 🚨 Active Threats

**UPDATE: Check Point releases the [CVE-2026-16232](../vulnerabilities/CVE-2026-16232/profile.md) fix and names 5 attacker IPs**
- 🔗 **Update on:** 2026-07-22 afternoon brief — the CVE moves from "KEV-listed, no vendor patch, zero IOCs" to fix-released with 5 detection IPs and a direct vendor exploitation attestation.
- Check Point published its own advisory, shipped a patch for the SmartConsole unauthenticated auth-bypass (an attacker obtains an admin login token), confirmed active exploitation affecting a small number of customers, and released 5 attacker source IPs for defensive log monitoring.
- **A&D relevance is structural.** SmartConsole sits at the identity/perimeter tier of many DIB estates; the fix makes the exposure window closable, not closed. Exploitation is **likely** to continue against the July 25 KEV deadline until the patch is deployed.

- Distinct from the Check Point Security Gateway flaw [CVE-2024-24919](../vulnerabilities/CVE-2024-24919/profile.md) — different CVE and component; do not conflate.

- **Action:** deploy the SmartConsole fix before July 25 if in federal scope; hunt the 5 IPs in management-console and perimeter logs — do not block on sight.

## 🇮🇷 Iran Cyber Watch

**UPDATE: SecurityWeek relays the CISA/FBI/EPA Iran-OT advisory with specific PLC models, ports, and named victims**
- 🔗 **Update on:** 2026-07-22 afternoon brief — a second publisher adds detection specificity to the same July 22 advisory revision.
- **Net-new detail:** targeted PLC models and config software — Rockwell CompactLogix/Micro850 (Studio 5000), Schneider Modicon M340 (EcoStruxure Control Expert), Siemens S7-1200 (TIA Portal); targeted ports 44818 (EtherNet/IP), 502 (Modbus), 102 (S7comm), 2222, and 22 (SSH); and two named victims — California Water Service and Stryker. - **Action:** DIB OT operators should inventory internet-facing Rockwell/Schneider/Siemens PLCs, restrict the named ports at the perimeter, and verify segmentation. Nothing here indicates A&D-specific targeting.

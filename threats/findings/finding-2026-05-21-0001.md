## Summary

SecurityWeek today (2026-05-21 09:52 UTC) publicly bound the two
Microsoft Defender zero-days that CISA added to KEV on 2026-05-20 to
the codenames "UnDefend" (CVE-2026-41091, LPE via CWE-59 link-
following, CVSS 7.8) and "RedSun" (CVE-2026-45498, DoS, CVSS 4.0),
sourcing MSRC. BleepingComputer independently corroborates the patch
versions (Microsoft Malware Protection Engine 1.1.26040.8 + Antimalware

codenames have been tracked in Archimedes `_index.yaml` since 2026-03
as unnamed, unCVEd zero-days (ZD-002 RedSun, ZD-003 UnDefend). The
new SecurityWeek binding **inverts** the type assignments recorded
two months ago — vuln-tracker reconciliation required before the
`_index.yaml` is updated with CVE bindings.

The types are **inverted** between the two records. Two

   source that originally named them inverted the association).

patched. Both are on CISA KEV with federal deadline 2026-06-03.

vulnerabilities that the vendor patched this week — vuln-tracker
maintenance pass is warranted regardless of the inversion outcome.

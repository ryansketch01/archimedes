# CVE-2026-41940 cPanel/WHM auth bypass — KEV-listed, federal patch deadline passed 2026-05-03

## Summary

CISA added CVE-2026-41940 (WebPros cPanel & WHM and WP2) to the KEV catalog on 2026-04-30 — a Missing Authentication for Critical Function flaw in the login flow that lets unauthenticated remote attackers gain access. Per The Record, exploitation grants full control over the cPanel host system, its configurations, databases, and the websites it manages. CISA mandated patching for federal agencies by 2026-05-03 under BOD 22-01; that deadline passed yesterday. A&D direct exposure is low — primes are unlikely to run cPanel for production hosting — but possible exposure exists on marketing microsites or contractor-facing portals. Surfaced now for analyst awareness; no new development overnight, hence non-FLASH at the 07:30 sweep.

## Technical detail

- **CVE:** CVE-2026-41940
- **Vendor/Product:** WebPros — cPanel & WHM and WP2 (WordPress Squared)
- **Class:** Missing Authentication for Critical Function (auth bypass in login flow)
- **Vector:** Remote, unauthenticated
- **Impact:** Full control over cPanel host system, its configurations, databases, and managed websites (per The Record paraphrase of CISA / vendor)
- **KEV addition:** 2026-04-30
- **Federal patch deadline:** 2026-05-03 (passed)
- **Required action per CISA:** Apply vendor mitigations; follow BOD 22-01 cloud-services guidance; or discontinue use.

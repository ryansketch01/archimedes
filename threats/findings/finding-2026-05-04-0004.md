# Trellix discloses breach of a portion of its source code repository; no customer or corporate data compromise reported

## Summary

Trellix confirmed on 2026-05-04 that attackers accessed a portion of its source code repository, per BleepingComputer relaying Trellix's updated statement. Trellix says it has found no evidence that corporate or customer data was stolen and no evidence its source code release or distribution process was affected. Investigation is ongoing with outside forensic experts; law enforcement has been notified. Detection date, intrusion vector, attribution, and the specific products whose code was touched are not disclosed. Trellix services roughly 50,000 business and government customers across approximately 200 million endpoints, including deployments in defense-industrial-base environments — making this a low-confidence-but-worth-tracking item under the security-vendor-source-code precedent that prior incidents (e.g., 2024 EDR vendor disclosures) preceded targeted detection-bypass engineering within 6–12 months.

# CISA ICS 5-advisory batch 2026-05-21 — ABB B&R Automation Studio CVSS 9.8 lead (24+ CVE deep-dependency batch 2015-2024 lineage); ABB B&R PCs + Runtime + Terra AC Wallbox + Hitachi Energy GMS600; Critical Manufacturing + Energy sector deployment; no active exploitation; defense supply chain ICS-layer adjacency

## Summary

CISA published a 5-advisory ICS batch on 2026-05-21 at 12:00 UTC (08:00 EDT — right at morning brief composition cutoff), covering four ABB B&R Automation product advisories and one Hitachi Energy GMS600 advisory. Lead advisory ICSA-26-141-03 ABB B&R Automation Studio carries CVSS 9.8 and is a 24+ CVE deep-dependency batch spanning 2015-2024 lineage updates (third-party component refresh). Sibling advisories cover ABB B&R PCs (9-CVE SinoCMS 2023 lineage), ABB B&R Automation Runtime (XSS + CSV formula injection + predictable identifiers, CVSS 6.1), ABB Terra AC Wallbox EV charger (buffer overflow chain, CVSS 6.1), and Hitachi Energy GMS600 (OpenSSL CVE-2022-4304 timing-side-channel, CVSS 5.9). Critical Manufacturing + Energy sector deployment. No active exploitation reported on any advisory; no KEV listing on any. ABB vendor acknowledges "no successful exploitation was observed during testing" while characterizing the identified vulnerabilities as potential attack vectors enabling unauthorized access, data exposure, or RCE. Defense supply chain ICS-layer adjacency: ABB B&R is widespread across DIB Tier-2/3 contract manufacturers running control systems for precision machining and small-batch assembly serving DIB Tier-1/2 customers.

- **Sector**: Energy (Critical Infrastructure)
- **Country of deployment**: Worldwide
- **Vendor HQ**: Switzerland
- **Affected versions**: B&R Automation Studio <6.5 (and 6.5 — some configurations)
- **CVE count**: 24+ CVEs spanning 2015-2024 (deep-dependency third-party-component refresh — CVE-2025-6965, CVE-2025-3277, CVE-2023-7104, CVE-2022-35737, CVE-2020-15358, CVE-2020-13632, CVE-2020-13631, CVE-2020-13630, CVE-2020-13435, CVE-2020-13434, CVE-2020-11656, CVE-2020-11655, CVE-2019-19646, CVE-2019-19645, CVE-2019-8457, CVE-2018-20506, CVE-2018-20505, CVE-2018-20346, CVE-2018-8740, CVE-2017-10989, CVE-2016-6153, CVE-2015-6607, CVE-2015-5895, CVE-2015-3717, CVE-2015-3416)
- **Pattern**: Deep-dependency third-party-component update; CISA characterizes as "An update is available that replaces an outdated third-party component"
- **Vendor statement (under 15-word quote ceiling, paraphrased)**: No successful exploitation observed during testing; identified vulnerabilities could present potential attack vectors enabling unauthorized access, data exposure, or RCE
- **CVSS**: 9.8 (highest in batch)

**Why this matters**: B&R Automation Studio is engineering software for the B&R control systems used in manufacturing — packaging, printing, plastics, food/beverage, semiconductor — but also defense-adjacent manufacturing (CNC, robotics integrators, contract manufacturers serving DIB Tier-2/3). The 24-CVE-deep batch is a single-product accumulator of unpatched dependency CVEs that engineering-workstation defenders must triage all at once.

# Morning Brief — 2026-06-11

**Ivanti Sentry CVE-2026-10520 (CVSS 10.0 pre-auth root RCE) transitioned from patched-no-ITW to early post-patch exploitation within ~24h of watchTowr's public PoC — Shadowserver scanned 19 Internet-exposed instances and confirmed 2 backdoored; FBI and DOJ seized 13 websites that officials allege Chinese intelligence services used to recruit US-cleared personnel via LinkedIn.**

**Why it matters:** Ivanti Sentry sits inline with ITAR-cleared mobile-device traffic to M365 GCC / GCC-High; any unpatched Internet-exposed instance is in the compromise window. The FBI/DOJ takedown targets the same cleared-personnel population A&D contractors employ — the attack surface is workforce, not network. Splunk Enterprise and PAN Cortex patches do NOT affect Frank's first-party stack but matter for A&D-prime SOCs.

## 🚨 Active Threats

**UPDATE: Ivanti Sentry CVE-2026-10520 — early post-patch exploitation observed**
- What: Shadowserver Foundation [attests "a large amount of Ivanti Sentry exploitation attempts based on the public PoC"](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-ivanti-sentry-vulnerability-now-exploited-in-attacks/) (13 words, Hard Rule 6); scanning surfaced 19 vulnerable Internet-exposed instances with **2 confirmed backdoored**. [watchTowr Labs published PoC 2026-06-10](https://labs.watchtowr.com/more-evidence-that-words-dont-mean-what-we-thought-they-meant-ivanti-sentry-pre-auth-os-command-injection-cve-2026-10520/) targeting `POST /mics/api/v2/sentry/mics-config/handleMessage`. The 19-source population likely includes benign scanners (watchTowr customers, ASM vendors, academics); the **2 backdoored hosts are the confirmed-compromise evidence**.
- Why it matters for A&D: Sentry is the MDM gateway in many ITAR mobile-device estates. Structural exposure; no first-party Frank instance and no A&D-prime victim named.
- Action: **Emergency patch to R10.5.2 / R10.6.2 / R10.7.1**; compromise-hunt Sentry access logs and perimeter proxy for the named endpoint; treat any unpatched Internet-exposed instance as in the compromise window. CVE-2026-10523 (CVSS 9.9 auth bypass) is in the same advisory and remains exploitable even though Shadowserver did not name it in this hour's telemetry.
- Source: Shadowserver Foundation (A provisional) + watchTowr Labs (B provisional) + [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/10/ivanti-sentry-cve-2026-10520-cve-2026-10523/) (B provisional) + BleepingComputer (B); Ivanti PSIRT advisory not yet updated to reflect ITW. - CVE-2026-0274 does NOT overlap the now-expired CVE-2026-0257 GlobalProtect Portal ITW lock — different CVE-ID, different product line.

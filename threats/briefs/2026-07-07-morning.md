# Morning Brief — 2026-07-07

**BeyondTrust patched four flaws in its Remote Support (RS) and Privileged Remote Access (PRA) products — two critical (CVSS 9.2) pre-authentication authentication bypasses that let unauthenticated attackers reach privileged appliances.** No in-the-wild exploitation is reported and no actor is attributed; ~2,000 instances sit internet-exposed per Shadowserver.

**Why it matters:** RS/PRA is privileged-access-management / identity infrastructure of a class the defense industrial base commonly runs. A pre-auth bypass on a privileged-access gateway is a chokepoint toward downstream systems — but the A&D relevance here is structural, not tied to any named victim.

---

## 🔓 Vulnerabilities

**[BeyondTrust RS/PRA — four CVEs, two critical pre-auth auth bypasses; patch now](../vulnerabilities/BeyondTrust-RS-PRA-BT26-03/profile.md)**

- What: BeyondTrust disclosed and fixed four vulnerabilities in Remote Support and Privileged Remote Access. NVD confirmed the ratings 2026-07-06:
  - **CVE-2026-40138** — CVSS 9.2 critical, pre-auth authentication bypass, RS + PRA ≤ 25.3.2 (requires a specific authentication configuration enabled).
  - **CVE-2026-40139** — CVSS 9.2 critical, pre-auth improper authentication, affects both RS and PRA.
  - **CVE-2026-40140** — CVSS 8.7 high, pre-auth denial-of-service.
  - **CVE-2026-40141** — CVSS 8.5 high, authenticated (low-privilege) authorization-scope bypass.
- Fix: upgrade to **25.3.3** (25.3.x branch) or **26.2.1** (26.2.x branch). Cloud customers were patched 2026-04-21; self-hosted / on-prem operators must upgrade themselves.
- Status: **not in CISA KEV; no active exploitation reported; no actor attributed.**
- Why it matters for A&D: RS/PRA is PAM/identity infrastructure common in the DIB, and a pre-auth bypass on this class of appliance is a privileged chokepoint. Relevance is structural — no A&D victim is named and no A&D targeting is reported.
- Action: DIB identity/PAM owners running self-hosted RS/PRA should inventory version and upgrade to 25.3.3+ / 26.2.1+ now. Treat as patch-now hygiene, not active-threat urgency — with the caveat that absence of an in-the-wild report is not proof of safety.
- Historical context only (Hard Rule 2): Silk Typhoon exploited earlier, unrelated BeyondTrust RS/PRA zero-days in the 2024–25 Treasury breach. No actor is attributed to CVE-2026-40138–40141; none is originated here. Silk Typhoon is not on the Archimedes roster — do not cross-walk to Salt Typhoon or Volt Typhoon.
- Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/beyondtrust-warns-of-critical-flaws-in-remote-access-software/) (relay of the BeyondTrust advisory) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely

# Polish ABW publicly attributes water-treatment ICS attacks across five municipalities to Russian special services; no tracked-actor named; A&D adjacency via Poland-as-aid-logistics-hub is inferential

## Summary

Poland's Internal Security Agency (ABW) has publicly disclosed cyberattacks on water-treatment industrial control systems across five Polish municipalities (Jabłonna Lacka, Szczytno, Małdyty, Tolkmicko, Sierakowo), per The Record (Alexander Martin, 2026-05-07 12:38 EDT). ABW frames a broader 2024-2025 campaign that also targeted the Polish national railway, air traffic control system, state news agency PAP, and energy infrastructure. Per ABW: attackers, in some cases gaining access to industrial control systems, "had the ability to alter technical parameters of devices" — describing a "direct risk" to water-supply continuity. ABW attributes the campaign to Russian state services with the published language: "with particular emphasis on the special services of the Russian Federation." Polish PM Donald Tusk warned of "ruthless" action against entities "directly or indirectly aiding Russian services." CyberDefence24 (relayed by The Record) links the activity to a "pro-Russian hacktivist group" not named in the available source text. **ABW does NOT publicly cross-walk to any tracked actor.** Sandworm (Archimedes roster id 007), APT28 (006), and APT29 (009) are NOT named by ABW. Archimedes does not originate the cross-walk on the basis of inferential TTP match (Sandworm-class playbook against Polish OT). No IOCs disclosed by ABW in this statement; no specific CVEs referenced; no A&D entity targeted (per source). Single-source-via-relay; single-source veto applied; WEP capped at "likely" on the operational claim. Promote at monitoring inclusion. A&D adjacency is INFERENTIAL via Poland-as-aid-logistics-hub for US/EU military aid to Ukraine — recorded for actor-profiler context on next refresh of Russian-actor dossiers (006, 007, 009).

## Technical detail

- **Targets named (water treatment):** Jabłonna Lacka, Szczytno, Małdyty, Tolkmicko, Sierakowo (Poland).
- **Targets named (broader 2024-2025 campaign):** Polish national railway, air traffic control system, state news agency PAP, energy infrastructure.
- **ICS impact framing (per ABW):** "Attackers, gaining access in some cases to industrial control systems, had the ability to alter technical parameters of devices." Interpreted: direct OT-process-control reach, not just IT-network compromise. The implication is operational-disruption capability, not just espionage.
- **Attribution (ABW, public-statement level):** "Russian special services" generally. ABW does NOT name Sandworm, APT28, APT29, or any specific tracked actor in the public statement.
- **Attribution (CyberDefence24, relayed by The Record):** "pro-Russian hacktivist group" — group not named in available source text.
- **IOCs:** none disclosed by ABW in this public statement. ABW has not made IPs, domains, malware family names, or hashes public in the relayed text.
- **CVE:** none referenced.

## A&D relevance — inferential, not source-stated

**No A&D entity is named as a target.** Polish military-aid logistics is mentioned as context (Poland as logistics hub for Western military aid to Ukraine), but no specific defense contractor, A&D prime, or watchlist entity is named in the source.

Inferential A&D-adjacency:

1. **Poland-as-aid-logistics-hub.** Poland is the principal land-route hub for US/EU military aid to Ukraine. A&D primes (Lockheed Martin Javelin and HIMARS deliveries, RTX Patriot deliveries) transit Polish logistics infrastructure routinely.
2. **Sustained-interdiction-of-aid logic.** Russian targeting of Polish OT (water, rail, air traffic, energy) maps to the same operational logic that historically drove Sandworm targeting of Ukrainian critical infrastructure — interdiction of aid flow, civilian-life disruption to pressure political support.
3. **Tier-1/2 supplier downstream risk.** A&D suppliers operating in or transiting Poland may face downstream OT-disruption risk if water/energy/rail are degraded.

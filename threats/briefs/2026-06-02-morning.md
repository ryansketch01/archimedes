# Morning Brief — 2026-06-02

**Google patches an Android Framework zero-day (CVE-2025-48595) under limited targeted exploitation; the Miasma npm wave gains four-vendor corroboration with an OIDC initial-access hypothesis; a Meta AI confused-deputy flaw exposes an AI-agent authorization class applicable to custom A&D LLM-RPA wrappers; ENISA's NIS360 2026 graduates Aviation to high maturity while Space stays in the risk zone.**

**Why it matters:** Executive Android patching ahead of OEM long-tail; DIB CI/CD owners face a T-8-day KEV deadline on a ~10M-download campaign; custom A&D AI wrappers get a new vulnerability class; European primes gain a sector benchmark.

## 🚨 Active Threats

**Miasma Mini Shai-Hulud npm wave — multi-firm corroboration; OIDC hypothesis; 210 downstream repos** *(campaign progression on [2026-06-01 afternoon](2026-06-01-afternoon.md) / [finding-2026-06-02-0003](finding-2026-06-02-0003.md))*

- **What.** [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/supply-chain-attack-hits-32-red-hat-npm-packages/) consolidates [ReversingLabs](https://www.reversinglabs.com/), [Aikido](https://www.aikido.dev/), [Ox Security](https://www.ox.security/), [Socket](https://socket.dev/blog) on the `@redhat-cloud-services` 32-package / 96-version compromise (~10M downloads). Aikido names GitHub Actions OIDC token-issuance abuse as the CI/CD vector; Ox enumerates 210 downstream repos; ReversingLabs reports a 72s window. **Hard Rule 2:** TeamPCP attribution is restatement (Socket declined, SecurityWeek aggregates); the 72s window does not distinguish CI/CD compromise from attacker-automation with stolen credentials (96 × ~0.75s ≈ 72s) — the OIDC hypothesis rests on Aikido alone.
- **A&D action.** Re-run `grep -r '@redhat-cloud-services/' package*.json` across CI/CD repos; rotate pipeline secrets. **CVE-2026-45321 (VT-006 parent) KEV deadline 2026-06-10 — T-8 days.** DFARS / CMMC do not mandate KEV inheritance; treat as elevated-urgency.
- Digraph **B2** · WEP **likely** · finding-2026-06-02-0003 · 🔗 extends [VT-006](threats/vulnerabilities/_index.yaml).

**Meta AI confused-deputy flaw — vulnerability class applies to custom A&D LLM-RPA wrappers** *([finding-2026-06-02-0002](finding-2026-06-02-0002.md))*

- **What.** [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/meta-ai-hands-over-high-profile-instagram-accounts-to-hackers/) and [Krebs](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/06/hackers-used-metas-ai-support-bot-to-seize-instagram-accounts/) independently report a chatbot confused-deputy weakness rebinding high-profile Instagram accounts via natural-language requests — Obama White House handle, Sephora, U.S. Space Force CMSF Bentivegna's personal account, hundreds unnamed. Meta patched; no CVE; no enterprise compromise. **Narrower framing:** the class generalizes to **AI integrations under privileged service identity** — custom A&D LLM-RPA wrappers and service-account AI integrations. Enterprise-AI on delegated-user-identity OAuth (M365 Copilot, Glean default) is structurally distinct; **Archimedes does not assert blanket exposure for those platforms** without per-deployment verification. Krebs's "pro-Iran hackers" is single-source (SecurityWeek does not echo); **Archimedes does not endorse the pro-Iran framing.**
- **A&D action.** Inventory internally-built LLM-RPA wrappers and service-account AI integrations; verify they cannot be coerced via natural-language to perform identity-management outside the invoking user's context.
- Digraph **B2** · WEP **very likely** procedural / **likely** attribution · finding-2026-06-02-0002.

## 🔓 Vulnerabilities

**CVE-2025-48595 — Android Framework LPE zero-day; limited targeted exploitation per Google** *([finding-2026-06-02-0001](finding-2026-06-02-0001.md))*

- **What.** [Google's June 2026 Android Security Bulletin](https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin), relayed by [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-fixes-one-actively-exploited-android-zero-day-124-flaws/), patches **CVE-2025-48595**, an Android Framework LPE flaw Google calls "limited, targeted exploitation." 124 fixes (18 critical + 105 high). **No CVSS, no mechanism** — Google holds for ~30 days. Pixels immediate; OEM long-tail 30–60 days. **Hard Rule 2:** Google does not attribute; BleepingComputer's "commercial spyware and nation-state operators" framing is pattern-match commentary, not co-attribution. ACH at **typology level only** ties commercial-spyware-class and spyware-vendor-to-state-customer at rank 1. **Archimedes preserves typology-class ambiguity.** A&D-executive applicability is **capability extrapolation**, not named-victim — public victim lists in this class name journalists, dissidents, ministers.
- **A&D action.** Prioritize executive / cleared-personnel Android fleets for the June update over OEM long-tail; verify Android Enterprise enforcement on corporate devices; assess BYOD exposure. Re-grade **2026-07-02** for Google's 30-day mechanism disclosure.

**ENISA NIS360 2026 — Aviation graduates to high maturity; Space stays in risk zone** *([finding-2026-06-02-0004](finding-2026-06-02-0004.md))*

- **What.** [ENISA's NIS360 2026](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/), relayed by [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/193002/reports/enisa-nis360-2026-progress-across-the-board-but-the-sectors-that-matter-most-are-still-falling-short.html), graduates **Aviation, Trust Services, FMI** to high maturity; **Space stays in risk zone**. Three dynamics: AI offense outpacing defense; supply-chain cascade; geopolitical / hacktivist intensification.
- **A&D relevance.** **European primes — [Airbus](https://www.airbus.com/), [Safran](https://www.safran-group.com/), [Thales](https://www.thalesgroup.com/) — operate under EU NIS2 directly.** US-prime relevance is indirect (ITAR-firewalled EU subsidiaries; EASA/FAA harmonization is safety-scope and does not carry NIS2 bands across; commercial-space cross-jurisdictional applies to SpaceX/Rocket Lab/Planet/Maxar, not classified US-prime space units). **Load-bearing US leg:** ENISA's supply-chain-cascade dynamic externally validates the [VT-006 / Mini Shai-Hulud / Miasma family](threats/vulnerabilities/_index.yaml) pattern (200+ packages). Do not echo aviation→space displacement — ACH ranks displacement last of five.

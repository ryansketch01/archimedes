## Active Threats

**UNC1549 expanded its recruiter-lure campaign onto US/UK/FR aerospace and defense primes (Feb–Apr 2026), per Mandiant.** Per [Mandiant / Google Threat Intel](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/unc1549-defense-recruiter-lure-2026/), IRGC-aligned UNC1549 (roster 004) used LinkedIn outreach plus lookalike careers portals (`defense-careers-portal.com`, `aerospace-talent-hub.net`) to deliver weaponized .lnk files staging MINIBIKE backdoor and MINIBUS loader against victims including "a major US space and defense contractor" and a European missile-systems integrator. Eleven IOCs published — C2 IPs 185.225.17.42 and 91.219.29.77, MINIBIKE/MINIBUS SHA-256s, recruiter persona email, Let's Encrypt 7-day TLS cycling. Mandiant is the only effective primary source (The Record and Kevin Beaumont relay only); single-source veto caps WEP at **likely**. **A&D action:** push the eleven IOCs through SIEM/EDR for a -90d hunt; brief HR, recruiting, and engineering on the pretext. Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely · finding-2026-05-05-0001.

**Charming Kitten / Mint Sandstorm ran a Q2 2026 OAuth-consent credential-harvest campaign against defense-policy think tanks (Brookings, RUSI, Atlantic Council).** Per [CrowdStrike](https://www.crowdstrike.com/blog/charming-kitten-thinktank-credential-harvest-2026-q2/) and [Microsoft MSTIC](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/mint-sandstorm-q2-2026-credential-harvest/), independently corroborated, IRGC-IO Charming Kitten (roster 011) used "comment on a paper draft" pretexts directing targets to OAuth phishing pages; MSTIC adds OAuth consent-grant tradecraft requesting Mail.Read + Mail.ReadWrite — persistence that survives password resets. **Scope discipline (red-team caveat):** the campaign as reported is bounded to think tanks, Iran-nuclear researchers, and MENA security journalists; **no defense prime is named as a victim.** Generalizing the OAuth tradecraft to prime mailbox risk is forward-looking and mechanism-based (Entra consent dialogs are platform-generic), not observed prime-direct activity. **A&D moves:** hunt Entra audit logs for unauthorized consent-to-application events on those scopes; confirm admin-consent-required posture for high-risk Graph scopes. Digraph: A1 · WEP: very likely · finding-2026-05-05-0002.

## Vulnerabilities

**Fortinet FortiManager CVE-2026-29841 — added to CISA KEV with the ransomware-known flag set; federal patch deadline 2026-05-25.** Per [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) and [Mandiant](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/fortimanager-cve-2026-29841-exploitation/), the authenticated FGFM RCE yields root on the management plane; observed post-exploit activity harvests managed-FortiGate configs (PSKs, BGP/IPSec material, admin password hashes). Patched: 7.6.4, 7.4.7, 7.2.10. **CVE record is very likely; active-exploitation intensity is likely, not very likely** — Mandiant scoped its observation to a small set of internet-exposed appliances, and CISA-Mandiant independence may share an upstream incident path (KAC A6); brief does not characterize the activity as widespread. Ransomware-known flag is reported as CISA's claim; no source names a specific crew (Hard Rule 2). **A&D priority:** FortiManager compromise hands an attacker the FortiGate fleet's keying material. Inventory FGFM (TCP/541) now; patch ahead of 2026-05-25. Digraph: A1 · WEP: very likely (CVE record) / likely (active exploitation) · finding-2026-05-05-0003.

**Microsoft IIS HTTP.sys CVE-2026-30445 — pre-auth RCE zero-day under "limited, targeted" exploitation; patch ships 2026-05-13.** Per [Microsoft MSRC](https://msrc.microsoft.com/blog/2026/05/may-2026-security-update-preview-cve-2026-30445/), the kernel-space pre-auth RCE via crafted HTTP/2 request affects Windows Server 2019, 2022, and 2025 with IIS + HTTP/2 enabled (default); CVSS 9.8. MSRC named no actor and published no IOCs. CISA acknowledged the notice but has not made an independent observation — single-source veto holds, WEP caps at **likely**. **8-day pre-patch window:** inventory internet-facing IIS / HTTP/2 today; patch 2026-05-13; where portals tolerate it, disable HTTP/2 via `netsh http set HTTP2 enabled=false`. Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely · finding-2026-05-05-0004.

**Cisco ASA / Firepower CVE-2026-20381 — patched same day; SANS reports 5x scanning spike against `/+CSCOE+/logon.html` within four hours.** Per [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-patches-critical-asa-firepower-vpn-flaw-cve-2026-20381/) relaying Cisco PSIRT and [SANS Internet Storm Center](https://isc.sans.edu/diary/cisco-asa-vpn-cve-2026-20381-mass-scanning), the pre-auth memory-corruption flaw in SSL-VPN/IKEv2 session handling yields unauthenticated RCE; CVSS 9.0. Fixed: ASA 9.20.4.5 / 9.22.2.3; FTD 7.4.3.2 / 7.6.1.1. SANS reports recon only — no exploit attempts in honeypots. Historical Cisco SSL-VPN baseline puts weaponization within 1–4 weeks of scanning. **A&D direct relevance:** ASA / Firepower is the remote-access VPN at most primes and Tier-1/2 suppliers, often the sole authenticated path for CMMC offsite engineers. Inventory now, patch within 7 days. **FLASH tripwire:** public PoC or first honeypot exploit attempt promotes this on the next sweep. Digraph: B2 · WEP: likely · finding-2026-05-05-0006.

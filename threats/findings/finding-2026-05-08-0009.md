# Polish ABW attributes five water-utility ICS breaches to APT28, APT29, and UNC1151; ICS-modify capability achieved at some sites

## Summary

SecurityWeek published "Polish Security Agency Reports ICS Breaches at Five Water Treatment Plants" on 2026-05-08T11:46 UTC, relaying a Polish Internal Security Agency (ABW) report documenting ICS intrusions at five water treatment facilities during 2025. ABW attributes primary responsibility to "hacktivist groups, though these are often personas used by foreign governments, particularly Russian intelligence services" and explicitly names APT28 (roster id 006), APT29 (roster id 009), and Belarusian-linked UNC1151 (not in roster — strong /new-actor candidate). Attack vectors per ABW: "weak password policies and systems exposed directly to the internet." Operational impact per ABW: "in some cases the attackers gained access to ICS and obtained the ability to modify the operational parameters of equipment, creating a direct risk to operational continuity and the public water supply."

This finding records the ABW attribution per Hard Rule 2 — Archimedes does not promote the attribution to canonical, only the procedural fact of ABW's attribution. The ICS-modify-capability claim against APT28 and APT29 specifically is non-canonical versus established GRU / SVR operational-doctrine models and warrants analyst SAT-ACH treatment. Polish water utilities are not direct A&D-target profile, but the same hygiene-failure modes apply to A&D-prime SCADA/OT environments.

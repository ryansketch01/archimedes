# Morning Brief — 2026-05-19

**Universal Robots PolyScope 5 cobot software carries an unauthenticated CVSS 9.8 RCE in its Dashboard Server — patched in 5.25.1, but A&D supplier-tier OT estates are the structural risk shape.**

**Why it matters:** Cobots run on Airbus, Boeing, and Lockheed Tier-1/2 supplier lines; OT-patch cadence lags IT by 6–18 months. Mini Shai-Hulud also posts one of its largest npm expansion days on record (639 versions / 323 packages).

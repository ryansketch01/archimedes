Analytic notes — read before briefing off this chart

01

"LOW" is often a weighting artifact, not low risk

Our threat-box weighting is espionage-heavy (35%). An OT-disruption actor (CyberAv3ngers) or a think-tank harvester (Charming Kitten) can post a category 🔴 HIGH yet weight to LOW overall. Prioritize on the per-category vector, not the overall label.

02

IRGC ≠ MOIS

Two services, not one org. Handala, MuddyWater, APT34 and Cavern Manticore are MOIS — do not brief them as IRGC. Overlaps (e.g. Scarred Manticore → Handala hand-off) are cited collaborations, never Archimedes-asserted merges.

03

The A&D-direct tier is small

Only UNC1549 and Peach Sandstorm carry A-grade documentation of prime-direct aerospace/defense targeting (Intent=5). Pioneer Kitten reaches defense contractors via access-brokering. Everyone else is structural or indirect exposure.

04

Candidates are proposals, not roster entries

The six dashed cards are well-documented in public reporting but not yet tracked, scored, or in the roster. Promoting any of them runs /new-actor — human approval, first-pass dossier, threat-box scoring, Hard Rule 5 gate on HIGH.

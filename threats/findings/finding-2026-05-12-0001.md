# SAP May 2026 Patch Day — Two Critical CVEs in Commerce Cloud and S/4HANA, No Exploitation Observed

## Summary

SAP published its May 2026 Security Notes on 2026-05-12 (Patch Tuesday)
addressing 15 vulnerabilities across multiple products. Two CRITICAL
flaws lead the batch: CVE-2026-34263 in SAP Commerce Cloud (missing
authentication enabling unauthenticated code execution) and
CVE-2026-34260 in S/4HANA (SQL injection enabling unauthorized
database access). SAP states no in-the-wild exploitation has been
observed at patch time and no specific customer is named as impacted.
Both CVEs are NVD-received but not KEV-listed. Structural A&D
relevance is high via S/4HANA deployment at scale across A&D primes
(Airbus, BAE Systems migration program ongoing; active migrations at
Lockheed Martin and Boeing) but no specific prime is named.

# CISA ICSA-26-125-03: Critical authentication bypass in Siemens SIMATIC S7-1500 / S7-1200 (CVE-2026-22118, CVSS 9.1, patches available)

## Summary

CISA published ICS Advisory ICSA-26-125-03 on 2026-05-05 disclosing CVE-2026-22118, an authentication bypass in Siemens SIMATIC S7-1500 and S7-1200 PLC families via a crafted PROFINET DCP discovery packet. CVSS 9.1 (Critical). Successful exploitation grants full PLC management plane access and arbitrary logic upload — i.e., the ability to reprogram the controller. Researchers at Claroty Team82 reported the issue privately to Siemens in February 2026 under coordinated disclosure (90-day timeline); Siemens released firmware 22.5.x (S7-1500) and 4.7.x (S7-1200) on the same day. **No in-the-wild exploitation observed at advisory publication.** Mitigations: segment PROFINET layer-2 traffic to engineering VLANs and disable DCP discovery on production segments where feasible. Direct OT relevance for A&D — SIMATIC controllers are widespread in machining, composites layup, and assembly-line automation across primes and Tier-1/2 suppliers.

## Technical detail

- **CVE:** CVE-2026-22118
- **Advisory:** CISA ICSA-26-125-03
- **Vendor / product:** Siemens SIMATIC S7-1500 and S7-1200 PLC families
- **Class:** Authentication bypass via crafted PROFINET DCP discovery packet → full PLC management plane access + arbitrary logic upload
- **CVSS (CISA / NVD):** 9.1 (Critical)
- **Affected configurations:** S7-1500 firmware <22.5 and S7-1200 firmware <4.7 with PROFINET DCP discovery enabled (default)
- **Patched versions:** S7-1500 firmware 22.5.x; S7-1200 firmware 4.7.x — released 2026-05-05
- **Disclosure timeline:** Reported privately by Claroty Team82 in February 2026; coordinated 90-day disclosure
- **Exploitation status:** No reports of in-the-wild exploitation at advisory publication
- **Mitigations (CISA):** Segment PROFINET layer-2 traffic to engineering VLANs; disable DCP discovery on production segments where feasible; apply firmware updates per vendor schedule.

# MacSync macOS infostealer surfaces via Google Ads malvertising abusing real Claude.ai shared-chat URLs

## Summary

BleepingComputer, relaying research from Trendyol Group security engineer Berk Albayrak, reports an active macOS malvertising campaign that abuses Google Ads sponsored placements for the search query "Claude mac download" and uses real `claude.ai/share/...` Anthropic shared-chat URLs as the in-lure instruction page. The terminal payload is a previously-undocumented macOS infostealer the research calls **MacSync**, delivered through a `curl | sh` shell-loader chain hosted on attacker-controlled infrastructure (`customroofingcontractors[.]com`, `bernasibutuwqu2[.]com`). Single-vendor research relayed by a single publisher; no second independent vendor confirmation in window; VirusTotal corroborates 3 of 5 IOCs as malicious. No threat-actor attribution made by the source. No A&D, defense, or DIB entity named as targeted — commodity-infostealer victim profile aimed at macOS users seeking Claude AI tooling.

## Technical detail

**Attack chain (per BleepingComputer relay of Trendyol research):**

1. Victim searches Google for "Claude mac download" or similar
2. Sponsored ad displays `claude.ai` as the visible URL but redirects through `customroofingcontractors[.]com`
3. Redirect terminates at a prepared `claude.ai/share/...` Anthropic Claude shared-chat URL
4. The attacker-prepared chat conversation walks the victim through executing a `curl | sh` command pointing at `customroofingcontractors[.]com/curl/<sha256>.daily`
5. The first-stage shell loader fetches a secondary loader from `bernasibutuwqu2[.]com/debug/loader.sh?build=<hash>`
6. The loader stages MacSync (macOS infostealer-class payload)

**Diagnostic novelty:** The use of real Anthropic-platform `claude.ai/share/...` chat URLs as an authority-signal landing page within the lure flow. Anthropic's shared-chat feature exposes user-generated chat contents at predictable URL paths; the attacker seeded adversarial-instruction chats on the real platform and treats them as the "official-looking" instruction page. **This is misuse of a legitimate generative-AI feature, NOT a compromise of Anthropic infrastructure.**

**MacSync family designation** is sourced from the Trendyol research only — VT engine tags read `shell` / `malware` without naming the family. Treat "MacSync" as the researcher's working designation, not vendor-consensus naming.

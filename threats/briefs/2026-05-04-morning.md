# Morning Brief — 2026-05-04

**A Linux kernel root-LPE ("Copy Fail," CVE-2026-31431) is being exploited in the wild per CISA KEV and Microsoft MSTIC, with federal agencies due to patch by 2026-05-15 — two more critical-class supplier-software flaws (MOVEit Automation, PyTorch Lightning) hit in the same 24-hour window.**

**Why it matters:** The three priority items each touch a distinct A&D attack surface — production Linux fleets, B2B file-transfer infrastructure, and ML/CI/CD supply chains — so single-control mitigations do not cover the morning's exposure. Patch windows on all three are short.

---

## Active Threats

**Linux kernel "Copy Fail" (CVE-2026-31431) — confirmed exploited, federal deadline 2026-05-15.** Per [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) (added 2026-05-01) and [Microsoft MSTIC](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/01/cve-2026-31431-copy-fail-vulnerability-enables-linux-root-privilege-escalation/) (same day), the `algif_aead` AF_ALG flaw enables unprivileged-to-root local privilege escalation across Ubuntu 24.04, Amazon Linux 2023, RHEL 10.1, SUSE 16, and Kubernetes nodes. Theori released a public reliable PoC the same day.
- **A&D operational reach** is concentrated where local-attacker reach is realistic: CI/CD runners, multi-tenant Kubernetes namespaces, and ML training clusters with researcher-tier accounts. Single-tenant production fleets are less exposed.
- **Forward framing is consistent with historical Linux LPE post-disclosure patterns** — opportunistic uptake within already-compromised hosts, with targeted incorporation by sophisticated operators plausible but not currently evidenced in this cluster.
- **Detection caveat:** Whether prime kernel-audit and EDR coverage tuned to AF_ALG / `algif_aead` misuse will catch exploitation cannot be independently validated by Archimedes (KAC A3, "test"). Treat "will appear in telemetry" framing accordingly.
- Digraph: A1 · WEP: almost certainly (active exploitation) · finding-2026-05-04-0001

**MOVEit Automation auth bypass (CVE-2026-4670, CVSS 9.8) — patches available, no in-the-wild exploitation reported.** [Progress Software](https://community.progress.com/s/article/MOVEit-Automation-Critical-Security-Alert-Bulletin-April-2026-CVE-2026-4670-CVE-2026-5174) disclosed the unauthenticated remote auth bypass plus CVE-2026-5174 (CVSS 7.7 privilege escalation), credited to Airbus researchers; per [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/moveit-automation-customers-warned-to-patch-critical-auth-bypass-flaw/), Daniel Card observed roughly 1,400 exposed Shodan instances with over a dozen tied to U.S. local and state government.
- **Most likely 30–90 day pattern (each at roughly even chance):** slow opportunistic uptake by lower-tier criminal operators, or selective targeting by an unattributed actor against high-value targets. Recurrence of the publicly-recorded 2023 Cl0p MOVEit Transfer mass-exploitation precedent is plausible as a worst-case template; current evidence does not support imminent recurrence, and the affected product (Automation, internal orchestration) has a different deployment surface than the 2023 product (Transfer, web-facing MFT).
- **CMMC framing:** potential CMMC-relevant exposure where Automation is deployed in supplier-facing flows. Whether A&D primes use it that way (vs. internal-only orchestration) is an open assumption anchored on the Airbus reporter signal alone.
- **High-priority tripwire:** public PoC release would flip the ranking toward opportunistic uptake — patch ahead of that.
- Digraph: B2 · WEP: likely · finding-2026-05-04-0002

**PyTorch Lightning 2.6.3 backdoor — "ShaiWorm" credential stealer, package reverted to 2.6.1.** Per [Microsoft Threat Intelligence via BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/backdoored-pytorch-lightning-package-drops-credential-stealer/), the malicious release activated on import, fetched Bun v1.3.13 from GitHub, and ran an 11.4 MB obfuscated `router_runtime.js` payload that harvests `.env` files, GitHub tokens, browser data, and AWS/Azure/GCP credentials, with arbitrary command execution. Lightning AI reverted the official package; pipeline-compromise mechanism is still under investigation.
- **"Small number of devices" affected per Microsoft Defender telemetry** — Linux ML infrastructure on alternative EDR and researcher Macs outside corporate visibility are likely undercounted.
- **Action where 2.6.3 was actually pulled AND stealer-chain network preconditions were met:** rotate GitHub PATs, cloud roles, browser-stored creds, and `.env` contents in the affected scopes. Not a blanket prime-wide rotation.
- **SBOM diff** across ML programs (`requirements.txt`, `Pipfile.lock`, `poetry.lock`) for any pull of `pytorch-lightning==2.6.3` is the right detection step.
- Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely · finding-2026-05-04-0003

## Vulnerabilities

- **CVE-2026-31431 "Copy Fail"** — Linux kernel `algif_aead` logic flaw; local vector, low privilege, no UI. Affected kernel range spans 2017 through patch date. CVSS not yet finalized at NVD; severity descriptors only. Federal BOD 22-01 deadline 2026-05-15.
- **CVE-2026-4670 / CVE-2026-5174** — MOVEit Automation auth bypass (CVSS 9.8) and privilege escalation (CVSS 7.7). Fixed in 2025.1.5, 2025.0.9, 2024.1.8 (full installer upgrade required; no in-place mitigation). No active exploitation reported.
- **PyTorch Lightning 2.6.3** — supply-chain compromise of the official build/release pipeline (not typosquat). No CVE assigned. Reverted to 2.6.1; no IOCs published yet.

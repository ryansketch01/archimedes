# Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS User-ID Authentication Portal zero-day CVE-2026-0300; active exploitation; no patch available

## Summary

Palo Alto Networks PSIRT issued an advisory 2026-05-05 for CVE-2026-0300, an unauthenticated pre-auth remote code execution vulnerability in the PAN-OS User-ID Authentication Portal (Captive Portal) on PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls. Palo Alto characterized it as the highest possible severity with CVSS 9.3 and stated that limited exploitation has been observed targeting internet-exposed portals. No patch is available for any branch at finding creation; vendor lists patch ETAs across May 2026 and offers two workarounds (restrict portal access to trusted zones, or disable the portal). BleepingComputer relays the vendor advisory and adds Shadowserver scan data showing 5,800+ VM-series firewalls internet-exposed, concentrated in Asia (2,466) and North America (1,998). No threat actor is named in any source. No IOCs are published. Single-source veto applies on the exploitation claim — Palo Alto is the only entity with telemetry on the in-wild activity, and BleepingComputer's reporting is a relay. WEP capped at "likely" pending an independent A/B-grade source confirming exploitation.

# Morning Brief — 2026-07-20

**Attackers are actively exploiting ServiceNow CVE-2026-6875** — a critical (CVSS 9.5) unauthenticated sandbox-escape to RCE in the AI Platform — with first attempts July 18, per threat-intel firm Defused relayed by BleepingComputer. The fix shipped July 13 (KB3137947), so exploitation postdates the patch.

**Why it matters:** ServiceNow is pervasive across the defense industrial base (ITSM, GRC, change management, service delivery), so an unauthenticated RCE is a real initial-access surface for DIB estates. No A&D targeting is claimed, the exploitation rests on a single provisional telemetry feed, and the CVE is not yet on CISA KEV — so this is a defensive-priority row, not a confirmed campaign against the sector.

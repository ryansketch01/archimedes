# ESET (via The Record): DPRK-attributed APT37 (Scarcruft) compromised Sqgame Android platform in supply-chain attack — ethnic-Korean targeting in Yanbian, China since November 2024

## Summary

ESET research, relayed by The Record (Jonathan Greig, 2026-05-07 00:12 EDT), reports a long-running APT37 supply-chain compromise of the Sqgame card-game platform. The compromise window opens at least November 2024; victims downloaded compromised game APKs from web browsers (not the Google Play store) and installed the BirdCall backdoor. BirdCall has both Android and Windows variants per ESET. ESET attributes the campaign explicitly to APT37 (a.k.a. Scarcruft, RedEyes, Reaper, Group123), DPRK-nexus and MSS-attributed per The Record's framing. Targeting: ethnic Koreans in the Yanbian Korean Autonomous Prefecture (China), "likely North Korean refugees or defectors" per ESET — civil-society / human-rights targeting consistent with DPRK regime objectives toward defector tracking and coercion. ESET notified Sqgame in December 2025; the article does not state a remediation date. **No A&D entity targeted; no defense-contractor or DIB victim named.** APT37 is NOT currently in the Archimedes _roster.yaml; the roster's DPRK actors (003 Lazarus / RGB and 002 Stardust Chollima / RGB-financial) are operationally distinct from APT37 (MSS) per public reporting. Recommendation: `actor-profiler` evaluate APT37 as a `/new-actor` candidate at next refresh — multi-vendor track record (ESET, Mandiant, Kaspersky, Cisco Talos historically), MSS attribution differentiating from existing roster DPRK actors, and mobile-malware capability differentiating from existing roster profiles together support roster-admission consideration. Single-source veto applied (single-vendor source); WEP capped at "likely." Promote at monitoring inclusion only — outside primary A&D scope but methodologically interesting for A&D-adjacent civil-society / NGO partner organizations.

## Technical detail

- **Compromised platform:** Sqgame (card-game platform). Exact domain not surfaced in The Record's article excerpt; passive DNS / VirusTotal enrichment recommended at actor-profiler stage.
- **Supply-chain window:** opens at least November 2024 per ESET; remediation date not stated in source.
- **Distribution path:** web-browser download of compromised game APKs from the Sqgame site (NOT Google Play store). This is significant — Google Play sideload-warning behavior is the user's only friction; consumer-grade Android security model otherwise relies on Play vetting which this attack bypasses by routing around the store.
- **Malware:** BirdCall (Android variant primary; Windows variant also exists per article).
- **Targeting:** ethnic Koreans in Yanbian Korean Autonomous Prefecture, China. ESET's framing: "likely North Korean refugees or defectors." Civil-society / human-rights targeting consistent with DPRK regime objectives.
- **Attribution:** ESET — APT37 (Scarcruft / RedEyes / Reaper / Group123). DPRK-nexus, MSS-attributed per The Record's framing. Distinct from Lazarus (RGB) and Stardust Chollima (RGB-financial) in the Archimedes roster.

## A&D relevance — none direct; methodological note only

**No A&D entity targeted; no DIB victim named.** This campaign is outside Archimedes' primary scope. Reasons to surface it briefly anyway:

1. **Methodological pattern.** Gaming-platform supply-chain compromise → mobile + Windows malware → diaspora/refugee targeting matches DPRK's broader civil-society coercion playbook. This is the pattern that A&D-adjacent civil-rights / NGO partner organizations (e.g., human-rights NGOs that interface with refugee-defector communities and may share threat intelligence with DIB CTI teams) should be aware of.
2. **APT37 historical A&D-adjacency.** Historical APT37 reporting (Mandiant, Kaspersky, Cisco Talos) includes occasional targeting of defense-think-tanks and policy-research organizations, which is A&D-adjacent. The Sqgame campaign itself is not A&D, but the actor's broader profile is worth tracking.
3. **DPRK roster gap.** Archimedes roster currently has only RGB-attributed DPRK actors (Lazarus, Stardust Chollima). APT37 (MSS) would close a structural gap if admitted via /new-actor.

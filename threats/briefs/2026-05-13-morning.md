# Morning Brief — 2026-05-13

**[UPDATE: yesterday's Patch Tuesday "Word RCE pair partner" CVE-2026-40361 is in fact a zero-click use-after-free in a DLL shared by Word and Outlook — exploitable via Outlook's preview pane with no user interaction, per Haifei Li / Expmon relayed by SecurityWeek.](https://www.securityweek.com/microsoft-patches-critical-zero-click-outlook-vulnerability-threatening-enterprises/)** Li explicitly equates the flaw to [CVE-2015-6172 (BadWinmail)](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/vulnerability/CVE-2015-6172), the 2015 Outlook preview-pane RCE he also disclosed; he built a PoC but not a working exploit, and Microsoft reports no in-the-wild exploitation at disclosure.

**Why it matters:** Outlook preview-pane zero-click is the highest-impact attack vector against M365 estates — every A&D prime mailbox sits on the exposed surface. The historical class (BadWinmail, Outlook NTLM, MonikerLink) has been weaponized by [APT28](../threat-actors/APT28/profile.md) and APT29 within 2–4 weeks of disclosure on prior cycles. Patch on the May 2026 Patch Tuesday cycle now; do not wait for the next maintenance window.

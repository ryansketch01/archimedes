## 🚨 Active Threats

**GlassWorm takedown — YARA rules and benign sinkhole published.**
- What: CrowdStrike Counter Adversary Operations + Google + Shadowserver Foundation disrupted GlassWorm's parallel C2 stack 2026-05-26 14:00 UTC. Infected machines redirect to CrowdStrike sinkhole `164.92.88.210`. YARA rules published for `GlasswormRAT` and `GlasswormDownloader`. Attack vectors: trojanized OpenVSX extensions, compromised npm + PyPI packages, 300+ poisoned GitHub repos via stolen developer credentials.
- Why it matters for A&D: the four-channel C2 design (blockchain + DHT + cloud-service + VPS) is reusable beyond GlassWorm — decentralized-resilience C2 should be treated as a growing class. Engineering-workstation IDE marketplaces are the load-bearing initial-access vector.
- Action: ingest both YARA rules into EDR; mark `164.92.88.210` benign-sinkhole in egress monitoring; audit OpenVSX / Cursor / Windsurf extension installs across engineering estates.
- Source: [CrowdStrike](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/inside-crowdstrike-takedown-of-a-developer-targeting-botnet/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/glassworm-botnet-disrupted/) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: very_likely (takedown, four-channel C2); likely (Russia-origin, single-source veto). **MSTIC — active cryptojacking on `gleeze[.]com` + Dynu DDNS; novel AI-chatbot-recommendation-poisoning lure.**
- What: [Microsoft Security Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/26/poisoned-search-results-gpu-mining-cryptojacking-campaign-abusing-screenconnect-microsoft-net-utilities/) (yesterday 17:35 EDT) disclosed 150+ malicious domains since March 2026: SEO poisoning + AI-chatbot poisoning → lookalike utility download → ZIP with legitimate utility + malicious `autorun.dll` → DLL sideload silently installs ScreenConnect → GPU mining. Nine `autorun.dll` variants; six impersonated brands (CrystalDiskInfo, HWMonitor, DDU, FurMark, K-Lite, PDFgear) selected for discrete-GPU audience. MSTIC attributes to "unknown threat actor".
- Why it matters for A&D — split layers: **IOC layer (action-tier)** — `gleeze[.]com`, Dynu DDNS, `autorun.dll`, ScreenConnect-in-unexpected-context are immediately defender-actionable. **A&D engineering-workstation indirect exposure (monitoring-tier, structural-warning class)** — target brands overlap PC-enthusiast audience with A&D engineering staff running CAD / simulation / GPU compute; exposure varies by estate maturity.
- Action: block `gleeze[.]com` and Dynu DDNS lookalikes; hunt ScreenConnect installs without enterprise-IT provenance on engineering workstations.
- Source: [MSTIC](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/26/poisoned-search-results-gpu-mining-cryptojacking-campaign-abusing-screenconnect-microsoft-net-utilities/) · Digraph: A2 · WEP: very_likely (campaign artifacts); likely (AI-chatbot-poisoning trajectory). Hard Rule 2: "unknown threat actor" preserved.

The vishing-Entra-SSO chain IS corpus-attested for [Scattered Spider (#013)](../threat-actors/_roster.yaml); Archimedes does NOT cross-walk ShinyHunters to Scattered Spider per Hard Rule 2.
- Action: phishing-resistant MFA on Entra; Salesforce data-export anomaly thresholds. Posture is sound regardless of which actor cluster is responsible at Charter.

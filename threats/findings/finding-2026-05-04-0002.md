# Progress patches critical MOVEit Automation auth bypass (CVE-2026-4670, CVSS 9.8); Airbus credited as reporter, Cl0p+MOVEit precedent looms

## Summary

Progress Software disclosed a critical (CVSS 9.8) authentication bypass in MOVEit Automation on 2026-05-04, plus a high-severity (CVSS 7.7) privilege escalation flaw, both privately reported by Airbus researchers. The auth bypass enables unauthenticated remote attackers to access the service backend command port interface in low-complexity, no-user-interaction attacks. Patches are available concurrent with disclosure (versions 2025.1.5, 2025.0.9, 2024.1.8); no in-the-wild exploitation reported as of disclosure. Daniel Card observed roughly 1,400 MOVEit Automation instances exposed on Shodan, with over a dozen tied to U.S. local and state government agencies. The Airbus reporter credit and 2023 Cl0p MOVEit Transfer precedent put this on the operational priority list for any A&D prime running MFT for B2B file transfer with suppliers.

- **Vendor:** Progress Software (MOVEit Automation product line).
- **Affected versions:** 2025.1.4 (17.1.4) and earlier; 2025.0.8 (17.0.8) and earlier; 2024.1.7 (16.1.7) and earlier.

# ⚡ FLASH: Adobe ColdFusion CVE-2026-48282 — max-severity unauthenticated RCE reported under active exploitation within hours of disclosure (n-day; patch out since July 1)

*2026-07-06 16:29 EDT · B2 · WEP: likely · TLP:CLEAR*

**What.** Adobe ColdFusion [CVE-2026-48282](../vulnerabilities/_index.yaml) — a critical-severity unauthenticated RCE (low attack complexity, no user interaction; publisher-framed max-severity, NVD/Adobe CVSS confirmation pending) — is reported under active in-the-wild exploitation, per [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-adobe-coldfusion-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks/). This is an n-day: Adobe shipped the fix on 2026-07-01. Two independent primaries relayed in the report attest exploitation — the KEVIntel honeypot network observed attacks under two hours after disclosure, and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (national CERT) separately confirmed ongoing exploitation. No actor is attributed; no victim is named.

**Caveats.**
- **CVSS unverified.** Max-severity is the publisher's framing; no NVD/Adobe PSIRT number was retrieved. Treat as critical-severity — not a confirmed 10.0 or 9.8.
- **Corroboration is real but single-channel.** KEVIntel and CCCS are genuinely independent (honeypot telemetry vs. government CERT), but both reach us through one B-grade relay; neither primary was directly retrieved. WEP held at likely on that gap.
- **A&D relevance is structural, not observed.** ColdFusion carries a DIB/government footprint, but no A&D-prime victim or sector targeting is named.

**Impact.** ~800 internet-exposed instances per Shadowserver; unpatched share unknown. Affected: ColdFusion 2025.9, 2023.20, and earlier. Unauthenticated RCE on an internet-facing app server is a direct route to initial access on any exposed estate, including DIB suppliers running government-facing ColdFusion apps.

**Action.** *Patch now* — Adobe's fix has been out since July 1. Inventory ColdFusion across owned and DIB-supplier estates today, apply the July 1 updates on internet-exposed instances, restrict management interfaces, and hunt web-tier logs for anomalous POSTs and unexpected ColdFusion child processes. First-party Splunk shows 0 sentinel hits and Frank is not known to run ColdFusion — silence is not disconfirming.

**Sources.** [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-adobe-coldfusion-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks/) (B — relay) · KEVIntel honeypot telemetry (primary; relay-conveyed, unretrieved) · Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (primary; relay-conveyed, unretrieved).

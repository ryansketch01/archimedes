## Summary

Socket and Aikido Security joint research, relayed by The Hacker News (Ravie Lakshmanan byline) on 2026-05-23, documents a supply-chain compromise of four PHP packages in the Laravel-Lang Composer organization (`laravel-lang/lang`, `/http-statuses`, `/attributes`, `/actions`).

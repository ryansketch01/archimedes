# Huawei VRP zero-day attributed by The Record to 2025-07-23 Luxembourg POST national-telecoms outage — 10 months post-incident — no CVE, no public Huawei advisory, no state-actor attribution

## Summary

The Record from Recorded Future News reports that a previously unexplained 2025-07-23 Luxembourg-wide telecoms outage at POST Luxembourg (state-owned national telecommunications operator) has been attributed to a zero-day vulnerability in Huawei enterprise router software running the VRP (Versatile Routing Platform) network operating system. Huawei stated it had never encountered the attack among any of its customers and had no ready-made solution; Huawei did not respond to The Record's detailed questions about why no public CVE was issued. The flaw remains unexplained and unpatched in any public capacity. Mechanism per source: specially crafted network traffic caused continuous restart loops on affected Huawei enterprise routers — a DoS-class consequence, not RCE or data exfiltration. The Record explicitly preserves "no evidence that an attack was specifically directed at POST Luxembourg as a chosen target" — no named state actor, no public advisory, no CVE 10 months post-incident.

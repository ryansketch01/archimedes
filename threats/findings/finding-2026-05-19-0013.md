# CISA ICS Day-139 batch — Siemens RUGGEDCOM APE1808 CVSS 10.0 (CVE-2026-0300) amplifies the post-federal-KEV-deadline-lapse posture of PAN-OS Captive Portal vulnerability to OT/ICS appliance class — plus ABB / ScadaBR / ZKTeco / Kieback & Peter advisories

## Summary

CISA Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency published five ICS advisories on 2026-05-19, 08:00 EDT (ICSA-26-139-01 through ICSA-26-139-05). The most consequential item is ICSA-26-139-02 Siemens RUGGEDCOM APE1808 Devices, which carries CVSS 10.0 (maximum severity) and is bound to CVE-2026-0300 — the same PAN-OS Captive Portal out-of-bounds-write vulnerability Archimedes has tracked since CISA KEV addition 2026-05-06 with BOD-22-01 federal compliance deadline 2026-05-09 LAPSED (now T+10 days post-deadline-lapse; finding-2026-05-14-0005 carry-forward chain). This is the load-bearing operational delta: the PAN-OS Captive Portal vulnerability is now confirmed deployed inside Siemens RUGGEDCOM APE1808 industrial-rugged appliances, dramatically expanding exposure surface from "PAN-OS firewalls in IT networks" to "PAN-OS firewalls embedded in OT/ICS appliances at industrial sites" — including DIB-tier supplier-facility deployments. Other day-139 advisories cover ABB CoreSense HM/M10 path traversal (CVE-2025-3465 CVSS 7.1), ScadaBR four-CVE cluster (CVE-2026-8602/8603/8604/8605 CVSS 9.1 unauth RCE), ZKTeco CCTV undocumented configuration port (CVE-2026-8598 CVSS 9.1), and Kieback & Peter DDC building controllers XSS (CVE-2026-4293 CVSS 5.3). No active exploitation claims in advisories; no tracked-actor attribution.

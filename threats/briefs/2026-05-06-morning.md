## Active Threats

**Palo Alto Networks: pre-auth RCE in PAN-OS User-ID Authentication Portal (CVE-2026-0300, CVSS 9.3) — vendor-disclosed limited exploitation, no patch.** Per the [Palo Alto Networks PSIRT advisory](https://security.paloaltonetworks.com/CVE-2026-0300), a buffer overflow in the User-ID Authentication Portal (Captive Portal) on PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls allows unauthenticated RCE as root via crafted packets. PSIRT's verbatim wording: "Limited exploitation has been observed targeting Palo Alto Networks User-ID Authentication Portals." Both modifiers — the prevalence cap (limited) and the targeting clause (portals exposed to untrusted IPs) — are operationally load-bearing. Affected: PAN-OS 12.1, 11.2, 11.1, 10.2 (specific version matrix in advisory). Patch ETAs span all of May 2026; vendor offers two workarounds — restrict portal to trusted zones, or disable. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/palo-alto-networks-warns-of-actively-exploited-firewall-zero-day/) relays PA without independent telemetry; Shadowserver counts 5,800+ VM-series instances internet-exposed (Asia 2,466, North America 1,998), but that figure measures global surface, **not** prime / DIB exposure. No actor named (Hard Rule 2). No IOCs published. No CISA KEV entry yet. Digraph: A2 · WEP: likely · finding-2026-05-06-FLASH-0001.

**Mitigation triage — answer this first:** is your User-ID Authentication Portal reachable from untrusted IPs? Mature DIB and prime estates default to trusted-zone-only here, in which case the vendor's workaround is already in place and the action drops to inventory-confirm and plan the branch-specific patch. If the portal is internet-facing (contractor BYOD captive flows, partner extranets), restrict to trusted zones today or disable until the patch ships. GlobalProtect-served remote-access flows are unaffected by this CVE.

7-Eleven, the US convenience-store franchise chain, confirmed an intrusion detected 2026-04-08 into systems storing franchisee documents, per SecurityWeek's Eduard Kovacs reporting 2026-05-18 at 07:25 EDT. The company's state-regulator filing narrowly scopes the impact to two Maine residents' personal information from franchise applications; ShinyHunters listed 7-Eleven on its leak site 2026-04-17 claiming theft of more than 600,000 Salesforce records, demanded ransom by 2026-04-21, and later offered the data for $250,000. SecurityWeek frames the incident as the latest in an ongoing ShinyHunters Salesforce-customer-targeting campaign active since mid-2025 against organizations including Instructure, Vimeo, Wynn Resorts, Vercel, and Medtronic — none of which are aerospace-defense primes. The campaign mechanism per SecurityWeek is phishing, abuse of third-party integrations, and customer-tenant misconfigurations, NOT a Salesforce platform vulnerability. No A-grade vendor (Mandiant / CrowdStrike / Unit 42 / MSTIC / Volexity / Talos / Symantec) has published cluster research on ShinyHunters as a discrete trackable entity, which is methodologically notable for a multi-year publicly-named criminal actor. This is the second named-enterprise-victim disclosure same morning following Grafana (finding-2026-05-17-0001 Coinbase Cartel ecosystem-lineage), but Hard Rule 2 + LEGAL-POLICY no-attribution-laundering prevents Archimedes from propagating the SecurityWeek companion-article multi-step relay chain "Coinbase Cartel linked to ShinyHunters, Scattered Spider, and Lapsus$" as direct attribution of either incident to Scattered Spider (roster #013 HIGH).

# Morning Brief — 2026-07-29

**Arista's actively-exploited, CVSS 10.0 VeloCloud Orchestrator flaw (CVE-2026-16812) hits its accelerated federal remediation deadline tomorrow, July 30 — the patch has been out since Monday, so any still-unpatched on-prem Orchestrator is now one day from both federal non-compliance and standing exploitation risk.** This is the one action-forcing item on an otherwise quiet, steady-state board.

**Why it matters:** VeloCloud Orchestrator is the SD-WAN control plane — one unauthenticated command-injection compromise pushes configuration and routing changes fabric-wide, so for an A&D enterprise this is a network-fabric exposure, not a single-appliance risk. The exposure is closable; the clock runs out tomorrow.

## ✈️ Sector Focus: Aerospace & Defense

**CubePilot — a defense-adjacent UAV flight-controller vendor — hit by DNS hijacking; firmware downloaded July 24-25 flagged as a supply-chain tampering risk**
- What: BleepingComputer relays CubePilot's own advisory that cubepilot.org was DNS-hijacked on July 24; the attacker obtained TLS certificates for all subdomains, enabling adversary-in-the-middle interception. CubePilot advised against flashing firmware downloaded July 24-25 pending verification (pre-July-24 firmware considered safe).
- Why it matters for A&D: CubePilot's "Cube" autopilots serve defense and government UAV applications and are supplied to Ukraine — a genuine supply-chain nexus. But the tampering is a vendor-flagged RISK, not a confirmed event; no downstream A&D or DIB victim is named; and the motive class is genuinely unresolved (targeted supply-chain, opportunistic domain hijack, and credential harvesting all fit the evidence equally — only hacktivism is refuted). Treat this as a supply-chain-integrity watch item, not a targeted campaign.
- Action: DIB UAV integrators running CubePilot Cube firmware should verify any build pulled July 24-25 against the vendor's guidance before flashing. The lone IOC (cubepilot.org) is the victim's own domain — do not blocklist.
- Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cubepilot-drone-software-dev-hit-by-dns-hijacking-to-intercept-traffic/) · Digraph: B2 (single-source veto — one relay of one vendor advisory). No actor named (Hard Rule 2).

## Summary

SecurityWeek (Kevin Townsend) relays Bitdefender Labs defensive trend research on MSHTA (mshta.exe) — Microsoft's HTML Applications binary introduced in 1999 — as a Living-Off-The-Land Binary (LOLBIN) for stealthy malware delivery. Per Bitdefender Labs relayed via SecurityWeek, a "dramatic rise in MSHTA-related activity" has been observed since the start of 2026, though specific volume metrics are not disclosed in the SecurityWeek article.
